The Transformers

Long before arriving on Earth, the Transformers were created on the metallic world of Cybertron. From Primus came the Thirteen Primes, whose leadership shaped early Cybertronian society. Over time, differences in ideology caused two factions to form: the heroic Autobots and the power-driven Decepticons. These tensions ultimately ignited the Cybertronian War.
